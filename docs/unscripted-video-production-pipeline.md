# Unscripted Video Production Pipeline

## Overview

This document defines the master template for the unscripted video production pipeline. It supports content creation workflows for podcasts, interviews, vlogs, and other unscripted video formats across eight stages spanning pre-production through distribution.

## Stage Definitions

### Stage 1 — Pre-production Planning
Define creative direction, content goals, scripted talking points (if any), shot lists, and schedule. Lock down the format, episode length, and distribution targets before any crew is booked.

**Key outputs:**
- Project brief / one-pager
- Content outline or question guide
- Production schedule
- Location/studio requirements

---

### Stage 2 — Talent and Crew Coordination
Confirm guests, hosts, and technical crew. Collect releases, distribute schedules, and align on logistics (travel, equipment, wardrobe, technical rider).

**Key outputs:**
- Confirmed talent list with release forms
- Crew call sheet
- Equipment checklist
- Logistics plan

---

### Stage 3 — Recording Sessions
Execute the recording according to the production schedule. Capture all primary footage, B-roll, and any supplementary audio.

**Key outputs:**
- Raw video files (organized by scene/segment)
- Audio tracks (separated where possible)
- Session notes / logging sheet
- Backup copies confirmed

---

### Stage 4 — Raw Footage Review
Log and review all captured footage. Identify the best takes, flag unusable material, and build a selects reel or rough assembly list for the editor.

**Key outputs:**
- Footage log with timecodes
- Selects reel or edit decision list (EDL)
- Notes on pacing, tone, and story arc
- Any re-shoot / pickup requests

---

### Stage 5 — Editing and Assembly
Cut the final piece from selects. Add titles, lower thirds, music, sound design, color grade, and any motion graphics. Deliver the first cut for review.

**Key outputs:**
- First cut (internal review)
- Revised cut(s) based on feedback
- Picture lock
- Audio mix and color-corrected export

---

### Stage 6 — Stakeholder Approval
Share the picture-locked edit with relevant stakeholders (client, sponsor, executive producer). Collect consolidated feedback and apply final revisions.

**Key outputs:**
- Stakeholder review link / screener
- Consolidated feedback document
- Final approved cut
- Signed-off deliverable checklist

---

### Stage 7 — Final Delivery
Export and package all deliverables to the required specs (resolution, codec, aspect ratios, captions, thumbnails). Transfer to the client or platform upload folder.

**Key outputs:**
- Master export file(s)
- Platform-specific versions (YouTube, social cuts, etc.)
- Caption/subtitle files (.srt / .vtt)
- Thumbnail(s) and promotional still(s)

---

### Stage 8 — Distribution Planning
Schedule and publish content across target platforms. Set up metadata, SEO copy, playlists, and any paid promotion. Monitor early performance metrics.

**Key outputs:**
- Platform publish schedule
- Video titles, descriptions, and tags
- Thumbnail A/B test (if applicable)
- Initial performance report (48–72 hrs post-publish)
