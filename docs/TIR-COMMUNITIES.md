# Third Industrial Revolution Communities

**Where the distributed energy, Economy of Things, and off-grid mesh crowd gathers — vetted join links, activity, and red flags.**

> **Status:** Research only, checked Nov 12, 2026. Nothing was joined and no messages were sent. Topics come from public information, not from inside the groups. Related: [Nyx platform overview](./NYX.md).

---

> **Method:** Everything was checked on **Nov 12, 2026, around 7:30 PM ET**. Discord numbers come from the public invite API (`discord.com/api/v9/invites/<code>?with_counts=true`). That returns `approximate_member_count`, and `approximate_presence_count`, which counts members **online** right then. Telegram numbers come from the public `t.me/<name>` preview page. I checked each invite against the project's own site, docs, or X account wherever I could, and the section notes name the source. Online/members is only a rough snapshot of activity. I joined nothing and posted nothing.
>
> I did not read message content in any group, because doing so would have meant joining. So "dominant topics" comes from each project's official descriptions, newsletters, and public posts, and should be treated as inferred, not observed.

## 3. OpenVPP

- **Official channel:** [t.me/OpenVPP](https://t.me/OpenVPP) ("OpenVPP 🌱 [Portal]"). [@OpenVPP on X](https://nitter.cf/OpenVPP/media) calls Telegram "the main channel for community-wide updates".
  - It has **3,859 subscribers** on Nov 12, 2026. An earlier search-index snapshot showed 4,093, so it's shrinking. *That earlier number is unverified as to date.*
- **The "over 1 GWh" claim is self-reported.** Posts say "over 1 GWh of tokenized flexible, dispatchable capacity and $30M in distributed energy assets as RWAs", through IP-license partner Grid Rails AI, on Arc/Circle and EVM ERC-1155.
  - **Not independently verified.** Their own homepage's live counters ([openvpp.energy](https://openvpp.energy/)) rendered as **0** devices, MWh, and kW when fetched.
- **Topics:** the $OVPP token, OpenVPP World (device registration and rewards), EV, thermostat, and battery demand response, agentic M2M nanopayments, and the Arc integration.
- **🚩 Red flags, self-disclosed in their [Oct 25 post](https://nitter.cf/OpenVPP/media):**
  - $OVPP was **delisted from MEXC**.
  - The team **cut headcount**.
  - The token only trades on Aerodrome now.
  - It's token-first marketing with little visible third-party verification of capacity.
  - **Verdict:** watch from a distance for market intel. Don't buy into a token on the strength of it.

## 4. DER Task Force

- **Join:** the Slack invite linked from the official [Resources page](https://www.dertaskforce.com/p/resources) ("expired link has been updated"): `https://join.slack.com/t/dertaskforce/shared_invite/zt-39kq8zyv9-Fa2hDyzZu9cII4ls2kNPsg`.
  - Slack invites expire. If it fails, use the current link on dertaskforce.com. Older links (`zt-23e8…`, `zt-2hxf…`) appear in podcast listings and filings and are probably dead.
- **Size:** "nearly 3,000 practitioners", self-reported in their [PA PUC comments](https://www.puc.pa.gov/pcdocs/1831584.pdf) from 2024. The current count is not visible.
- **What it is:** an "independently operating cooperative of DER practitioners". It isn't funded by member companies. It files crowdsourced PUC comments (for example FERC Order 2222 implementation in PA), and runs a podcast, a [Substack](https://www.dertaskforce.com/), monthly city [meetups](https://www.dertaskforce.com/p/meetups) (NYC, Bay Area, Seattle, Chicago, Toronto), and the DERVOS conference.
- **Topics (from the [newsletter](https://www.dertaskforce.com/p/der-task-force-april-newsletter-55d)):** EV charger pricing and franchise rights, grid altruism, off-grid generation legality, interconnection, VPPs, and policy filings.
- **People (public):** founders Duncan Campbell, Colleen Metelitsa, and James McGinniss, with Russell Wilcox as a founding member ([Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/fun-stuff/friday-social-meet-the-task-force-fighting-for-distributed-energy-resources)). James McGinniss publicly promotes DERVOS.
- **Verdict:** the highest-signal community on the list for real-world DER and policy. No token.

## 5. Work on Climate

- **Join:** the official site's join button goes to [bit.ly/joinWoCl](https://bit.ly/joinWoCl), which leads to a Typeform intake (`5sovtpfwgg0.typeform.com/intake`). That's the "3-min intake survey" described on their [newsletter About page](https://workonclimate.substack.com/about), after which you get a Slack invite. Signing up directly at workonclimate.slack.com needs an admin invite.
- **Size:** "40,000+ people", self-reported on [workonclimate.org](https://workonclimate.org/). That's well above the "10k+" in the brief. The Slack-specific count is not visible.
- **Topics:** climate jobs and career changes, founders and hiring, mentorship, local chapters, and topic channels (see the [Slack Channel Directory](https://workonclimate.notion.site/Slack-Channel-Directory-fa9afb9dda624630bfba94852c694d0b)).
- **People (public):** Eugene Kirpichov, co-founder and executive director ([About](https://workonclimate.org/about-us/), [Aspen Ideas](https://www.aspenideas.org/speakers/eugene-kirpichov)).
- **Verdict:** broad and career-oriented. Its energy-specific channels are useful, but the overall signal on distributed energy is lower than DERTF.

  - **53,450 members, 8,977 online** (16.8%), the most active server checked.

### MeshCore
- **⚠️ The project split in April 2026** ([MeshCore blog, "Why The Split?"](https://blog.meshcore.io/2026/04/23/the-split)). The core developers moved to **meshcore.io** and a new Discord. The original meshcore.co.uk site and Discord stayed with "Andy".

  - It's linked from [meshcore.io](https://meshcore.io/), the blog, and the [GitHub README](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore).
- **Splinter server:** meshcore.co.uk links discord.gg/QCgdTmuWUA. That guild (`1343693475589263471`, the *original* MeshCore server ID cited in old READMEs) is **now named "MeshOS"**, with 10,101 members and 1,311 online. Old invites (`BMwCtwHj5V`, `H62Re4DCeD`) are expired.
- **People (public, from the README and blog):**
  - Scott Powell (ripplebiz), the original developer.
  - Liam Cottle, who maintains the MeshCore app.
  - The blog names "Andy" as controlling meshcore.co.uk.
- **Topics:** firmware, repeaters, regional presets, handhelds, and the governance dispute.
- **Verdict:** join the meshcore.gg server. Know there's community drama, and avoid taking sides publicly.

## 🚩 Red flags

1. **`discord.gg/peaqnetwork` is a lookalike "peaq" server.** It has 557 members and a 2026-era guild ID, and peaq's site and Community Hub don't link it. The real server is `discord.gg/UKTFkPWsyH`, with about 136.6k members. Assume impersonation, which commonly leads to fake airdrops and wallet drainers. peaq says to verify contracts only at [peaq.xyz/token](https://www.peaq.xyz/token).
2. **OpenVPP:**
   - The 1 GWh and $30M RWA claims are self-reported only.
   - The site's live counters showed 0.
   - The token was delisted from MEXC, and the team cut headcount.
   - Subscribers are falling.
   - Token-first, so don't buy in.
3. **MeshCore split:**
   - Two servers both claim the brand.
   - meshcore.co.uk's Discord is now "MeshOS".
   - Old invites are expired.
   - Use meshcore.gg, as linked by the GitHub repo.
4. **Dead official invites** that leave room for scammers:
   - Arkreen's `discord.gg/WZKzdty8` and Glow's `discord.gg/glowfnd` are both "Unknown Invite".
   - Anyone can claim those vanity codes, and any "Arkreen" or "Glow" Discord DM'd to you should be treated as suspect.
5. **Low-signal, token-heavy chats:** peaq Discord (1.4% online), peaq Telegram, and the CPIN, Arkreen, and Combinder token groups. Expect airdrop and quest farming. **Never connect a wallet or sign anything from a link posted in chat.** Admins never DM first.
6. **Expiring Slack invites:** DER Task Force's old links in podcast listings and PUC filings are stale. Always take the link from dertaskforce.com.

## Suggested join order

1. **DER Task Force Slack.** It has the highest policy and practitioner signal, plus PUC comment drives.
2. **Energy Web Discord.** The most serious Web3-energy community, with an enterprise and utility audience.
3. **Meshtastic Discord**, then **MeshCore (meshcore.gg)**. Both are very active, with hands-on off-grid infrastructure.
4. **LF Energy Slack** and the **OpenEMS forum**, for open-source grid and DER software.
5. **Work on Climate** (intake form) and **ClimateAction.tech** Slack, for the climate network, hiring, and audience.
6. **peaq Discord (UKTFkPWsyH only)** in lurk mode, plus the **Combinder Telegram**, for Economy of Things and DePIN intel. Mute the general channels.
7. **Helium** and **Home Assistant** Discords, for DePIN precedent and the home-energy maker crowd.
8. **OpenVPP Telegram** in watch-only mode, and **The Great Simplification Hylo** for the macro and Rifkin-style narrative.
