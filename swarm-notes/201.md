## What was done
Added a new "Talent & Crew Coordination Sheet" template to the `talent_crew_coordination` stage of the `unscripted_video_production` lifecycle in `apps/api/src/db/lifecycle-templates.ts`.

The template (sortOrder: 2) complements the two existing templates for this stage:
- sortOrder 0: "Talent Profile & Availability" (pre-existing)
- sortOrder 1: "Crew Call Sheet Template" (pre-existing)
- sortOrder 2: "Talent & Crew Coordination Sheet" (NEW)

- Team Roster (on-screen talent, key crew, additional crew)
- Availability Matrix with day-of-week grid and status key
- Contracts & Agreements (talent contracts, crew deal memos, release forms tracker)
- Logistics (travel, accommodation, ground transportation, per diem/expenses)
- Assignment Finalization Checklist
- Emergency Contacts
