## Budget Considerations

- **June is brutal** — 5 conferences across 4 countries/cities. Consider cutting 1-2.
- **San Francisco repeats** — 5 conferences there. Get a monthly coworking space or Airbnb for October.
- **Early bird registration** — most conferences offer 20-40% off if you register 3+ months early.
- **Booth vs. attendee** — for Tier 1 conferences (GitHub Universe, AI Engineer Summit), a booth/sponsor slot is worth the investment. Attend-only for Tier 3.
